Scoring Algorithm

How Jobs Are
Scored & Ranked

A weighted multi-factor algorithm that scores each job from 0 to 100.

100 Max Score
🛠️ Skills Match 35%

Compares your skills against job requirements. Fuzzy matching handles variations like "JS" vs "JavaScript".

💼 Title Match 25%

How well the job title matches your current/desired titles. Handles seniority levels and title synonyms.

📍 Location 15%

Matches preferred cities, states, and remote work preference.

📊 Experience 10%

Compares your years of experience with the job's requirements.

🔑 Keywords 10%

Must-have and exclude keywords boost or penalize the score.

💰 Salary 5%

Checks if the listed salary fits within your desired range.

85-100 Excellent Match
70-84 Great Match
55-69 Good Match
40-54 Fair Match
0-39 Low Match
